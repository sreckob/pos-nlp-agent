Agentic RAG for quality-grading node

1.⁠ ⁠Dispute policy / chargeback regulation retrieval — highest value
[5/9/26, 13:25:13] Srećko Srecko Batalov: The approver agent currently validates with hardcoded rules (same day, same amount, < 30 min, different approval code). In real payment systems, chargeback rules are complex and brand-specific: Visa Dispute Resolution has different rules than Mastercard's or local Dinacard. Those rules live in dense   
  PDF documents (PSD2 directives, card scheme operating regulations). 
  RAG over that corpus would let the approver pull the relevant clause when writing its audit summary — "applicable rule: Visa Dispute Resolution § 11.1.3, duplicate transaction within 30 minutes" — making the audit output genuinely     
  regulatory-grade rather than just pattern-matched.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                             
                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                 
  Implementation fit: Supabase already has pgvector available. 
  You'd embed the regulatory documents, store in a dispute_policies vector table, 
  and query at approval time: embed the dispute scenario → retrieve top-3 relevant clauses 
  → pass as context to DISPUTE_APPROVAL_PROMPT.
